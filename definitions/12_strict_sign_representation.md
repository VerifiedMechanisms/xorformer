# Definition 1.12. Strict sign representation

Let $n\in\mathbb{Z}_{\geq1}$ and $f:\lbrace0,1\rbrace^n\to\lbrace0,1\rbrace$.

A function $g:\lbrace0,1\rbrace^n\to\mathbb{R}$ is a **strict sign representation** of $f$ if it [classifies (Definition 1.3)](03_classification.md) $f$.

A real polynomial $P\in\mathbb{R}[x_1,\ldots,x_n]$ **strictly sign-represents** $f$ when its restriction to the Boolean cube is a strict sign representation of $f$.
