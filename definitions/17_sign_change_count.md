# Definition 1.17. Sign-change count

Let $f(x)=F(|x|)$ be a [symmetric function (Definition 1.16)](16_symmetric_functions.md). Its **sign-change count** is the number of adjacent weights with different labels:

$$ C(F)=\left|\lbrace k\in\lbrace1,\ldots,n\rbrace:F(k-1)\neq F(k)\rbrace\right|. $$

Write $\sigma_k=2F(k)-1\in\lbrace-1,1\rbrace$ for the target sign at weight $k$, for $0\leq k\leq n$.
