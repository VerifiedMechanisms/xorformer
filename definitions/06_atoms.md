# Atoms

For $n\in\mathbb{Z}_{\geq1}$, an **atom** $\phi$ is a function on $\lbrace0,1\rbrace^n$ of the form

$$ \phi(x)=\frac{\eta+\sum_{i=1}^{n}\rho_i\alpha^{x_i}(m_i+\delta x_i)}{\gamma+\sum_{i=1}^{n}\rho_i\alpha^{x_i}}, \qquad \gamma,\alpha,\rho_i\gt0, \quad \eta,\delta,m_i\in\mathbb{R}. $$

All parameters are fixed real numbers.
