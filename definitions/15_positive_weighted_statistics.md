# Definition 1.15. Positive weighted statistics

Let $n\in\mathbb{Z}_{\geq1}$ and let $\lambda=(\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_n)\in\mathbb{R}^n$ satisfy $\lambda_i\gt0$ for every coordinate. The corresponding **positive weighted statistic** is the function $t:\lbrace0,1\rbrace^n\to\mathbb{R}$ given by

$$ t(x)=\sum_{i=1}^{n}\lambda_i x_i. $$

Write $T$ for its set of attained values and $M$ for the number of these values:

$$ T=\mathrm{Im}(t)=\lbrace t(x):x\in\lbrace0,1\rbrace^n\rbrace, \qquad M=\lvert T\rvert. $$
