# Definition 1.19. Parity

Let $n\in\mathbb{Z}_{\geq1}$. Using [Hamming weight (Definition 1.16)](16_symmetric_functions.md) $|x|$, the **parity function** $\mathrm{PARITY}_n:\lbrace0,1\rbrace^n\to\lbrace0,1\rbrace$ is

$$ \mathrm{PARITY}_n(x)=\begin{cases} 1 & |x|\text{ is odd}, \cr 0 & |x|\text{ is even}. \end{cases} $$

We also write $\mathrm{XOR}_n=\mathrm{PARITY}_n$.
