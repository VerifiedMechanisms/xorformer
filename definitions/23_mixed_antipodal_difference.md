# Definition 1.23. Mixed antipodal difference

Let $p,q\in\mathbb{Z}_{\geq1}$ and let $g:\lbrace-1,+1\rbrace^p\times\lbrace-1,+1\rbrace^q\to\mathbb{R}$.

The **mixed antipodal difference** of $g$ is the function $\Delta g$ on the same domain given by

$$ \Delta g(x,y)=g(x,y)-g(x,-y)-g(-x,y)+g(-x,-y). $$

Here $-x$ and $-y$ negate every coordinate of the corresponding block.
