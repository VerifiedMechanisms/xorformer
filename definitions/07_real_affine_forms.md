# Definition 1.7. Real affine forms

Let $n\in\mathbb{Z}_{\geq1}$. A **real affine form** is a function $\ell:\mathbb{R}^n\to\mathbb{R}$ of the form

$$ \ell(x)=\beta_0+\beta^\top x=\beta_0+\sum_{i=1}^{n}\beta_i x_i, $$

where $\beta_0\in\mathbb{R}$ and $\beta\in\mathbb{R}^n$ are arbitrary. Constant forms are allowed by taking $\beta=0$, including the zero form when $\beta_0=0$.
