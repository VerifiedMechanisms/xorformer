\title{Exact shared repair of arbitrary row exceptions}
\maketitle

\paragraph{Kernel-checked statement.}
For every $k$-bit row $x_0$ and arbitrary real amplitudes $c_j$ in $s$
independent columns, there is a reverse cube--star bank of
$k(k+1)+1$ heads whose score is exactly $c_j$ at $(x_0,j)$ and exactly zero
at every other row. Every denominator is positive on the whole Boolean
cube, every local slope is strictly negative, and the column axis is
exactly zero. The declaration is
\texttt{HeadComplexity.AxisZeroGluing.jointFamily\_point\_repair}
in \texttt{JointFamilyPointRepair.lean}.
The declaration \texttt{jointFamily\_point\_family\_repair} combines $m$
exceptional rows at cost $m(k(k+1)+1)$, independently of the number of
columns.

\paragraph{Construction.}
Give coordinate $i$ weight $1$ when $(x_0)_i=1$, and $k+1$ otherwise.
The statistic $v(x)=\sum_i w_i x_i$ takes integer values between zero and
$k(k+1)$, and $v(x)=v(x_0)$ only at $x=x_0$. Indeed any outside coordinate
would contribute $k+1$, larger than $v(x_0)\leq k$; with outside
coordinates absent, equality forces every coordinate of $x_0$ present.
Let $Q$ be the Lagrange basis polynomial of the distinguished integer
$v(x_0)$ among these $k(k+1)+1$ levels. The inherited barycentric identity
writes a scaled version of $Q(v(x))$ as
\[
 \sum_{h=0}^{R}\frac{a_{hj}}{T+h-v(x)}
   = P_j(v(x))\prod_{h=0}^{R}\frac{T+h}{T+h-v(x)},
 \qquad R=k(k+1).
\]
Choosing $P_j$ as $c_j Q$ divided by the product at $x_0$ gives the exact
indicator values. On the complete row--column Boolean cube use
\[
 D_h(x,z)=T+h+1-v(x)-\sum_jz_j,
 \qquad N_h(x,z)=\sum_j a_{hj}z_j,
 \qquad T>\sum_iw_i+s.
\]
These formulas certify all denominator, slope, and axis conditions, not
just their restrictions to the required one-hot columns.

A sharper written count uses weights $1$ and $a+1$, where $a=|x_0|$.
Its number of distinct levels is $(a+1)(k-a+1)$, at most $(k+2)^2/4$.
The checked declaration uses the simpler uniform quadratic bound above.

\paragraph{Consequence for approximation routes.}
If a proposed joint decoder has squared Frobenius error $E$ from a sign
matrix, every row containing an incorrect or zero sign contributes at
least one to that error. Thus fewer than $E+1$ rows require repair. The
exact indicator bank repairs all their independent column values at a
quadratic cost per row, leaving all prior correct row signs unchanged.
For a block weak learner of cost $L$ and squared normalized correlation
$\gamma^2$, the usual matching-pursuit estimate therefore gives a
head budget of order
\[
 \frac{L}{\gamma^2}\log\frac{Ms}{E}+E k^2.
\]
In the balanced regime $M=2^k$, $s\asymp k$, choosing
$E=M/k^3$ makes the logarithm $O(\log k)$ and the repair term $O(M/k)$.
A benchmark-sized budget $O(M/\sqrt{k})$ would follow from a uniform
block gain
\[
 \frac{\gamma^2}{L}\gg\frac{\sqrt{k}\log k}{M}.
\]
No such uniform gain is proved here. This accounting removes the need
to drive the total error below one, but fully charges all exceptional
rows and every head in the weak learner.
