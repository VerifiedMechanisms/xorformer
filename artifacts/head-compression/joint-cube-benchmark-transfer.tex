\paragraph{Status.}
The transfers in this note are kernel checked. The decoder existence
hypothesis is open. In particular, this note does not assert an unconditional
power saving or an unbounded improvement to the logarithmic saving.
The original head model and worst-case complexity definition are unchanged.

\paragraph{General gain.}
Write $n=k+s$, suppose $s\geq16$ and $n\leq3s$, and let $0<g\leq n$.
If a reverse cube-star compiler uses at most $M$ heads for every independently
labelled table on $\{0,1\}^k\times[s]$, and
$Mg\leq A2^k$ with $A\geq0$, then
\[
 W(n)\leq(66A+1)\frac{2^n}{ng}.
\]
The input to this statement includes positivity of every local denominator
on the whole Boolean cube, strict negative local slopes, and exact zero
column-axis values. The existing gluing theorem supplies original-model
atoms, preserves prior strict signs, and charges the exceptional face.
Its initial count is $22\cdot2^sM/s+2^{k+1}$.
For the main term use $n\leq3s$.
For the exceptional term, induction gives $18s^2\leq2^s$ for $s\geq16$,
so $2ng\leq2n^2\leq18s^2\leq2^s$.
This proves the displayed constant without discarding exceptions.

\paragraph{Permutation tangent target.}
The checked weighted permutation tangent construction uses two banks,
so a local budget $Hg\leq A2^k$ gives
\[
 W(k+s)\leq(132A+1)\frac{2^{k+s}}{(k+s)g}.
\]
All row directions, pole locations, affine perturbations and permutation
catalogs may depend on the complete family of independently chosen labels.
They must be common within that family in exactly the way specified by
\texttt{HasCompactWeightedPermutationTangentDecoder}. The decoder is required
for every positive compactness width, so its conversion pays the full-cube
column margin rather than just the one-hot margin.

Take $k=\lfloor n/2\rfloor$ and $s=n-k$. For every $n\geq32$, the explicit
remaining sufficient condition is
\[
 H\sqrt{k}\leq A2^k,
 \qquad
 \texttt{HasCompactWeightedPermutationTangentDecoder}(k,s,H).
\]
It would imply
\[
 W(n)\leq(264A+2)\frac{2^n}{n\sqrt n}.
\]
Indeed $n\leq4k$ gives $\sqrt n\leq2\sqrt k$.
This reduction identifies the exact missing existence theorem; neither
feature rank, finite successful label samples, nor the size of a permutation
catalog proves that theorem.
