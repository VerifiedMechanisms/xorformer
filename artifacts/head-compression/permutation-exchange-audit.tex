\paragraph{Scope.}
Permuting a column of denominator coefficients is a coupled modification
of several heads. Its combinatorial information count does not establish
a decoder on the Boolean cube. The following calculation audits the
simplest local modification, with exact values rather than a linearized
optimization objective. It does not exclude general coordinated changes
of many heads.

\section{Two-row transpositions}

\begin{proposition}[Exact two-row exchange]
Let $u+a,u+b,v+a,v+b$ be positive real numbers. Then
\[
 \frac1{u+a}+\frac1{v+b}-\frac1{u+b}-\frac1{v+a}
 =\frac{(a-b)(u-v)(u+v+a+b)}
 {(u+a)(u+b)(v+a)(v+b)}.
\]
In particular, its strict sign is the strict sign of $(a-b)(u-v)$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Group the two terms involving $u$ and the two involving $v$.
Putting the resulting two fractions over a common denominator leaves
\[
 (b-a)\big((v+a)(v+b)-(u+a)(u+b)\big)
 =(a-b)(u-v)(u+v+a+b).
\]
The denominator is positive. Also
$u+v+a+b=(u+a)+(v+b)>0$, proving the sign assertion.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[A coefficient transposition is affine on its active face]
Let $U,V$ be positive affine functions independent of a Boolean coordinate
$x_i$, and let $a,b>0$. Consider two positive-slope heads with common
constant numerator one and denominators $U+a x_i$ and $V+b x_i$.
Transpose just these two coefficients to obtain denominators $U+b x_i$
and $V+a x_i$. The old score minus the new score is zero on $x_i=0$.
On $x_i=1$ its sign is the sign of the affine function
$(a-b)(U-V)$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
On the zero face the denominators have not changed. On the one face
apply the preceding identity pointwise. All four denominators are
positive. Positivity of the original other slopes and of $a,b$ also
preserves admissibility after the transposition.
\end{proof}

Consequently such a single transposition cannot prescribe arbitrary
strict correction signs on a face of dimension at least two. For example,
the checkerboard signs on any coordinate square are impossible: affine
values $q_{00},q_{01},q_{10},q_{11}$ satisfy
$q_{00}+q_{11}=q_{01}+q_{10}$, which rules out positive diagonal and
negative off-diagonal values. This concerns the correction alone.
Adding a nonzero preexisting score changes the classification problem.

\paragraph{Unequal and affine numerators.}
Holding two arbitrary affine numerators $A,C$ fixed gives, on $x_i=1$,
\[
 \frac A{U+a}+\frac C{V+b}-\frac A{U+b}-\frac C{V+a}
 =\frac{(b-a)\big(A(V+a)(V+b)-C(U+a)(U+b)\big)}
 {(U+a)(U+b)(V+a)(V+b)}.
\]
The numerator restricted to this face has degree at most three.
For constant $A,C$ it has degree at most two. These identities follow
by the same subtraction, with no approximation. They show precisely
which additional interactions are available when numerators differ;
they do not bound a sequence of globally interacting exchanges.

The two scalar identities and the positivity equivalence are kernel
checked as \texttt{HeadComplexity.reciprocal\_exchange\_identity},
\texttt{reciprocal\_exchange\_pos\_iff}, and
\texttt{affine\_numerator\_exchange\_identity} in
\texttt{Results/ReciprocalExchange.lean}.

\section{A three-row cycle with a quadratic sign}

The preceding affine-sign obstruction does not extend to all cycles.
The following construction uses a third row to produce a strict
checkerboard correction while retaining the exact zero on the inactive
face.

\begin{proposition}[An exact three-cycle identity]
For $u,v>0$, define
\[
 D(u,v)=\frac1{u+1}+\frac1{v+2}+\frac2{u+v+6}
       -\frac1{u+2}-\frac1{v+3}-\frac2{u+v+2}.
\]
Then
\[
 D(u,v)=
 \frac{(u-v)(u-v-2)(u^2+4uv+v^2+13u+11v+22)}
 {(u+1)(u+2)(v+2)(v+3)(u+v+2)(u+v+6)}.
\]
Consequently $D(u,v)$ has the sign of $(u-v)(u-v-2)$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Put the six terms over the displayed common denominator and expand.
The resulting numerator is the stated product. Every denominator
factor is positive, as is $u^2+4uv+v^2+13u+11v+22$.
\end{proof}

To interpret this as a coefficient cycle, let $U,V$ be positive affine
forms in coordinates other than a selector bit $z$, and put
$W=(U+V)/2$. The old three denominators are
$U+z,V+2z,W+3z$, and the new denominators are
$U+2z,V+3z,W+z$. All six numerators are one. On $z=0$ the score
does not change; on $z=1$ the old score minus the new is $D(U,V)$.
If all other slopes of $U,V$ are strictly positive, every displayed
denominator is admissible.

\begin{proposition}[A strict checkerboard update]
For Boolean $x,y,z$, take
\[
 U=2+3x+y,\qquad V=1+x+3y,\qquad W=\tfrac32+2x+2y.
\]
Let $\Delta$ be the old score minus the new score under the above
cycle. Then $\Delta(x,y,0)=0$, and
\[
 (1-2x)(1-2y)\Delta(x,y,z)\le-\frac z{2100}.
\]
Thus the correction is strictly positive at $(x,y)=(0,1),(1,0)$
on the active face and strictly negative at $(0,0),(1,1)$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The denominators have positive constants and strictly positive slopes
in all three coordinates. The equality on $z=0$ follows directly.
On $z=1$, $U-V=1+2x-2y$, so the sign follows from the three-cycle
identity. Its four exact values, ordered as $00,01,10,11$, are
\[
 -\frac1{90},\qquad\frac{89}{16380},\qquad
 \frac{89}{16380},\qquad-\frac3{6188}.
\]
Their absolute values exceed $1/2100$, proving the margin assertion.
\end{proof}

The identity, sign equivalence, and displayed margin are kernel checked
as \texttt{reciprocal\_three\_cycle\_identity},
\texttt{reciprocal\_three\_cycle\_pos\_iff}, and
\texttt{reciprocal\_three\_cycle\_checkerboard} in the same Lean module.
The mathematical positivity check for the six affine denominators is
given above; it is not separately packaged as an attention-model
realization theorem in that module.

\paragraph{Compatibility limitation.}
This operation changes an existing three-head score. Viewed as a
standalone difference it uses six heads. Its correction is a signed
real quantity, not an XOR operation on an arbitrary existing score:
that score's active-face magnitudes must still be controlled. Moreover,
the averaging relation $W=(U+V)/2$ constrains every nonselector
coefficient. Subsequent overlapping cycles can destroy that relation.
There is no proved schedule of compatible cycles realizing all truth
tables at the target head count. The checkerboard result justifies
examining three-row interactions, without supplying that missing
global construction.

\section{Arithmetic permutation identities}

\paragraph{Number-theoretic permutation constructions.}
Ai, Hu and Yan, \emph{On Permutations Whose Adjacent-Sum Reciprocals
Sum to One}, \url{https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.18431}, Theorem 1.1,
construct permutations with one prescribed reciprocal sum. Their
Proposition 1.2 splices two edges with denominator $D$ into one edge
with denominator $D/2$, preserving the sum because
$-1/D-1/D+1/(D/2)=0$.
Propositions 1.4 and 1.6 preserve prescribed denominator multiplicities
under an inductive expansion. These give explicit arithmetic
cancellations, but do not prescribe independent signs at all $2^n$
input vertices. A simultaneous affine encoder and an admissible decoder
with the requested head budget remain missing.

\paragraph{Status.}
Independent two-row switches do not implement arbitrary local updates.
The explicit three-row cycle escapes the affine-sign restriction.
General cycles and changes of several coefficient columns remain
outside that obstruction. No universal construction from them is
claimed here, and the permutation counting heuristic is not treated
as an existence proof.
