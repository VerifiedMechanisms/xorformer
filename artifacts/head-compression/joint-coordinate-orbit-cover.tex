\section{An explicit coordinate-orbit cover}
This is a conditional transfer, not a new local decoder or a new asymptotic
bound. It removes an unnecessary combinatorial covering-design hypothesis.
Let $Q$ be a nonempty subset of the weight-$k$ layer of the $n$-cube, with
$e=|Q|$. Suppose every labeling of $Q$ has a limiting score of $H$ original
admissible heads that is zero on every higher layer and on the complement
of $Q$ in the weight-$k$ layer. Assume $1\le H\le e$.

Take all coordinate permutations of $Q$, retaining repetitions as indexed
cells. Coordinate permutations act transitively on the weight-$k$ layer and
preserve the original head class exactly. Hence every point has the same
positive incidence degree $d$, and double counting gives
\[
 n!\,e={n\choose k}d.
\]
The regular partial-cover theorem, with one-head singleton exceptions,
then produces a score for the entire layer with head count at most
\[
 {n\choose k}\frac{H}{e}\left(1+\log\frac eH\right).
\]
Every selected cell is compiled for its own pulled-back labeling; no labels
or decoder coefficients are identified across different orbit members.
The logarithmic loss is the logarithm of the local gain $e/H$, not the
logarithm of the number of points in a cell. Descending layer assembly
preserves earlier signs because each local score vanishes above its layer.

The exact checked declarations are
\texttt{coordinateOrbit\_regular},
\texttt{supported\_cell\_coordinate\_orbit}, and
\texttt{exists\_layer\_score\_of\_supported\_cell}, all in namespace
\texttt{HeadComplexity.AxisZeroGluing}, file
\texttt{formalization/HeadComplexity/Results/CoordinateOrbitLayerCover.lean}.
Their transitive axioms are only the standard
\texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice}, and \texttt{Quot.sound}.
The original model, admissibility conditions, and worst-case quantity are
unchanged. The missing constructive hypothesis is a supported cell with a
sufficiently large ratio $e/H$.
