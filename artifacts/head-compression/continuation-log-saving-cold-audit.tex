\paragraph{Finding.}
I found no mathematical or model-admissibility defect in the claimed
unconditional bound
\[
 W(n)\leq2323202\frac{2^n}{n\log n}\qquad(n\geq32),
 \qquad W(n)=O\!\left(\frac{2^n}{n\log n}\right).
\]
This conclusion follows from an independent reading of the construction,
the downstream gluing and approximation arguments, the original head model,
and a new exact-rational implementation of the individual head formulas.
The numerical experiment described below is diagnostic evidence only.
The general theorem is the Lean declaration
\texttt{HeadComplexity.worstCaseHeadComplexity\_real\_le\_log\_saving}
and its asymptotic counterpart in
\texttt{Results/UniversalLogSaving.lean}.

\section{The local construction has the stated information and head budgets}
For $q=2^k$, the encoding uses an injection of requested rows into
$(\mathbb F_q^2)\times\{1,\ldots,k\}$. Extension along this injection
allows arbitrary, independent labels on every requested row and column.
For a fixed point and column the resulting $k$ labels are encoded by one
field element. There is no linearity restriction on these Boolean words.
The finite-field cardinality is proved using the actual field of order
$2^k$; positivity of $k$ is supplied before that fact is used.

The Cauchy interpolation poles and tokens are fixed independently of the
target table. The pole and token intervals are disjoint, and both indexed
families are injective. The source proves the decoder by Lagrange
interpolation and a checked partial-fraction identity. It permits an
additional scalar constant for each bit; this becomes a row addition in
the rank compiler and is included in the single shared correction head.
Thus the difference between this implementation and a pure-reciprocal
matrix-inverse presentation adds no uncounted head.

The $q^2$ line aggregates are real constants calculated from the complete
target table. For a query point $h$, the sum over its nonvertical line
pencil contains the point $h$ once for each slope. A distinct point with
different first coordinate occurs on exactly one pencil line. A distinct
point with the same first coordinate occurs on none. This is true also
in characteristic two. The finite-sum proof separates these cases
explicitly and has no assumption of distinct row points: several bit
queries may use the same point.

The line matrix has $q^2$ displayed rank factors. Its exact rank compiler
uses $q^2$ heads and one shared affine correction. There is one additional
Cauchy correction head for each of the $q^2$ points. Hence the raw budget
is exactly $2q^2+1$, independently of the number of data columns. The
point corrections remove the whole off-diagonal term, not merely an
approximation to its sign. The remaining grid output is exactly $+1$ or
$-1$ for every prescribed table entry.

\section{Whole-cube positivity and strict slope signs}
Write $N=r+s$ and choose $\delta=1/(4(N+1))$. The correction denominator
has the form
\[
 1+\sum_i(D_i-1)u_i-\sum_jT_jv_j,
 \qquad 1-\delta<D_i<1,
 \qquad 0<T_j<\delta.
\]
Every variable coefficient is strictly negative, including coefficients
at rows where the numerator is zero. Its value is greater than
$1-N\delta>3/4$ on the entire Boolean cube. The proof does not restrict
positivity to the weight-two grid.

For a rank factor $f_i g_j$, the rank compiler chooses
$C=1+\sum_i|f_i|$, $u_i=C+f_i$, and $M=4(r+1)C$. Its denominator is
\[
 1-\sum_i\frac{u_i}{M+u_i}u_i^{\mathrm{row}},
\]
and its numerator is $M\sum_jg_jv_j$. The grid value is
$(M+C+f_i)g_j$. The omitted column term $(M+C)g_j$ is subtracted by
the one shared affine head. Since $0<u_i<2C$, the total denominator
decrease is less than $r/(2(r+1))<1/2$. Arbitrarily large or negative
entries of the rank factors cause no denominator failure.

The rank heads initially have nonpositive slopes. If $m$ is the value
of such a denominator at the all-one input, then $m>0$ and that input
is its minimum on the cube. The exact strictification is
\[
 D'(x)=D(x)+\epsilon\left(2-\sum_i x_i\right),
 \qquad \epsilon=\frac{m}{N+1}>0.
\]
Every new slope is strictly negative, the full-cube lower bound is
$3\epsilon>0$, and each weight-two grid value is unchanged. This also
handles the shared denominator-one correction without an extra head.

\section{Axis repair, gluing, and strict signs}
The axis repair removes the constant part of each ratio by writing
$A/D=(A-(A(0)/D(0))D)/D+A(0)/D(0)$. Its three-head construction
subtracts the two pure-axis restrictions. The substituted denominator
slopes are a common negative upper bound on the old slopes, so the new
denominators remain positive on every cube point. The induced grid
error is a row addition plus a column addition. Three more heads
repair the total row and column additions, including any original
scalar bias. Thus the exact total is $3H+3$ heads, all with strict
negative slopes and exact zero sum on both entire Boolean axes.

In the global construction a center reverses its selected coordinates.
Each reversed denominator has constant coefficient $D(\mathrm{center})>0$.
Its selected slopes are the negatives of the local negative slopes;
outside slopes are set to $1$. Adding the nonnegative localization
penalty keeps every ambient slope strictly positive. This is essential:
the argument never treats an arbitrary mixed-sign affine denominator
as an original-model head.

The descending-center geometry is sufficient for compatibility. An
earlier protected product-star vertex inside a later downset is on
one of the later axes or on the later product star. Its signed
contribution is therefore zero or positive. Outside the downset, each
fixed numerator divided by a denominator with an increasing positive
penalty tends to zero. Finiteness lets the proof choose a finite
penalty that preserves all earlier strict margins after choosing the
new module's positive scale. No limiting object is counted as a head.

The cover leaves the two faces where an entire block is one. The face
repair uses arbitrary real residual values, so it applies to the
previously constructed score. The second repair is based on the score
after the first, including their intersection. Each face is expanded
into at most $2^a$ or $2^b$ signed monomials and approximated by actual
atoms. The complete ideal score has a positive minimum absolute margin
on the finite cube. Choosing the total approximation error below that
margin preserves strict positive and negative outputs. Approximate
face realization therefore introduces no zero-margin exception.

\section{The original attention model is actually reached}
The resource is the original number of heads in
\texttt{computableWithHeadsN}; $H^{\ast}$ is its least feasible value,
and $W(n)$ is the maximum of $H^{\ast}$ over all Boolean functions.
The upper-bound path constructs actual heads before invoking the
least-value bound, so it does not exploit the fallback value in the
definition of $H^{\ast}$ for an unrepresentable function.

For clarity, an ambient ratio with numerator $a_0+\sum_i a_ix_i$ and
strictly coefficient-positive denominator $b_0+\sum_i b_ix_i$ uses
\[
 s=1+\frac{\sum_i b_i}{b_0},\quad
 \rho_i=\frac{b_i}{s},\quad \alpha=1+s,\quad
 \gamma=b_0-\sum_i\rho_i>0,
 \qquad m_i=\frac{a_i}{b_i},\quad
 \eta=a_0-\sum_i\rho_i m_i.
\]
Then the fractional atom with token-value increment zero has exactly
the desired numerator and denominator. In particular $\alpha$ is one
common token multiplier for every position in the head. The construction
does not silently replace the shared token structure by independent
per-position multipliers.

\texttt{FracAtomHead} explicitly realizes this atom by a width-three
attention head with a common scalar readout. Logarithms of the positive
weights determine the score coordinates, and the value and query-marker
coordinates are separate. One atom costs one head. The existing
shared-embedding bridge also preserves the number of heads, if literal
shared embeddings are required; its larger width is allowed by the
head-count invariant.

Every apparent product in the affine-plane line sums or rank factors
is a product of parameters fixed from the table. The actual numerator
and denominator of every ratio are affine in the original input bits.
No extra Boolean input, product gate, intermediate attention layer,
or determinant-evaluation operation is used. The construction does
require unrestricted real coefficients and precision, which the
original model permits.

\section{Finite padding and the explicit asymptotic constant}
For $r\geq5$, the least $k$ with $r\leq k4^k$ satisfies
$k\geq2$ and $k4^k\leq8r$. The finite encoding restricts directly
to the requested rows, so the larger capacity is not charged as
additional input coordinates. For $r=\lfloor n/2\rfloor$, the proof
establishes $\log n\leq5k$. It allows the slightly larger raw budget
$2\cdot4^k+2^k+1$, then proves the repaired budget at most
$600n/\log n$. The actual smaller compiler supplies this hypothesis.

The product cover has at most $484\,2^n/(ab)$ modules for
$a=\lfloor n/2\rfloor$ and $b=n-a$. The inequality
$n^2\leq8ab$ gives the factor $3872=484\cdot8$. For every $n\geq32$,
the proof establishes $n^2\leq2^{\lfloor n/2\rfloor}$, hence
\[
 2^a+2^b\leq\frac{2\cdot2^n}{n^2}
 \leq\frac{2\cdot2^n}{n\log n}.
\]
The final coefficient is exactly $3872\cdot600+2=2323202$.
The final theorem supplies the finite compiler argument explicitly;
the conditional interfaces in its dependencies do not remain as
premises of the public theorem. Natural logarithms are used, and
$n\geq32$ supplies their required strict positivity.

\section{Independent exact-rational stress test}
The new script \texttt{audit\_affine\_plane\_full\_heads.py}
implements the individual ratios separately from the Lean source.
It checks all $256$ Boolean tables of size $4\times2$ with $q=2$.
Each has nine raw heads and thirty repaired heads. It also tests
eighteen $q=4$ cases: all-zero, all-one, and seeded-random labels
at dimensions $(0,0)$, $(0,2)$, $(2,0)$, $(1,1)$, $(3,2)$, and
$(5,3)$, with shuffled restricted row embeddings.

All arithmetic is rational. The script evaluates both raw and repaired
scores on the grid, the repaired scores on every axis input, and
every raw and repaired denominator on every input of the tested cube.
It then reverses centers, adds a finite localization penalty, constructs
the original fractional-atom parameters with one common $\alpha$, and
checks its value against the affine ratio on every cube input. On
the center downset it also verifies equality with the original local
ratio after the deletion substitution.

The recorded result in \texttt{audit\_affine\_plane\_full\_heads.json}
is: $274$ tables, $4228$ exact grid comparisons, $5324$ exact axis-zero
comparisons, $760881$ full-cube denominator positivity checks, and
$583626$ reversed atom comparisons, all passing. No counterexample
was found. The universal result still rests on the source proofs;
these finite checks specifically target indexing, sign, cancellation,
and head-normalization mistakes that a summary could conceal.
