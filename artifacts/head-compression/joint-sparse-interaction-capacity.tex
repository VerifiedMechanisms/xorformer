\title{Sparse interaction does not remove the logarithmic budget}
\author{}\date{24 November 2026}\maketitle

\paragraph{Checked statement.}
Fix an input set of size $M$, a real parameter space of dimension $P$, and
an active head set $S_x$ for each input, with $|S_x|\le D$. Consider
\[
 F_\theta(x)=B_x(\theta)+\sum_{h\in S_x}
 \frac{A_{xh}(\theta)}{Q_{xh}(\theta)},
 \qquad Q_{xh}(\theta)>0.
\]
All numerators and the bias have polynomial degree at most $a$, and the
denominators have degree at most $b$. Coefficients and parameters may be
chosen jointly after the entire label family is known. The incidence sets
are fixed for this first statement. Clearing only the active denominators
gives degree at most $d=a+bD$. If $0<P\le M$, $d>0$, and every strict sign
pattern is represented, the kernel-checked Warren bound gives
\[
 2^M\le (4edM/P)^P,
 \qquad M\le 6P\log(4ed).
\]
The declarations are \texttt{sparseRatio\_universal\_capacity} and
\texttt{sparseRatio\_universal\_log\_budget} in
\texttt{JointSparseInteractionCapacity.lean}. Warren's theorem is already
unconditional in the base repository, through
\texttt{warrenStrictSignPatternBound}; no counting hypothesis is added here.

\paragraph{Charge label-dependent selection.}
For a finite library of $T$ architectures, each with the same parameter and
degree budgets, all labels may choose their architecture independently.
The proved union bound and logarithmic consequence are
\[
 2^M\le T(4edM/P)^P,
 \qquad M\le 6\log T+6P\log(4ed).
\]
These are \texttt{polynomialLibrary\_universal\_capacity} and
\texttt{polynomialLibrary\_universal\_log\_budget}. Thus combinatorial
selection must be charged, but it need not be confused with the active
degree. A library with $\log T=O(nH)$ and $P=O(nH)$ still gives only
$O(nH\log n)$ labels when $a,b,D$ are polynomial in $n$.

\paragraph{Consequence for the search.}
Within this class, an $H=O(2^n/n^{1+\delta})$ universal construction requires
$\log d=\Omega(n^\delta)$. An improvement
$H=O(2^n/(n\log n\,g(n)))$ requires
$\log d=\Omega(\log n\,g(n))$. For bounded numerator and denominator degree,
this forces respectively exponentially many or superpolynomially many
active interactions per query. Merely enlarging the domain to exponential
size while retaining a polynomial active neighborhood does not suffice.
These asymptotic substitutions are written consequences of the displayed
checked inequalities; they are not new bounds for unrestricted $W(n)$.

\paragraph{Scope and remaining possibilities.}
Unrestricted xorformer scores have all $H$ terms available at every input,
so this lemma does not rule out the benchmark. A near-face limiting model
must not silently drop tiny tails: diverging signed residues can retain
their effect or create high-order jets, increasing the effective degree.
The bound applies to the exact retained-term family, or to an approximation
only after a uniform error-versus-margin argument has justified that
replacement. Dense signed cancellation, label-adaptive high-degree
families, and larger architecture libraries remain possible.
