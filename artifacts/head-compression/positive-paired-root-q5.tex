\title{Two Positive Paired-Root Words Need Not Be Compatible}
\author{}\date{}
\begin{document}\maketitle
\paragraph{Status and scope.}
This is a finite exact obstruction to a proposed positive-residue
interpolation principle. Its rational certificate is checked without
an optimization solver. It is not a lower bound for the original
HeadComplexity model, and does not concern the fast hierarchical pole
grid used by the butterfly decoder. No Lean verification is asserted.

Put $p_r=9r/100$ for $0\leq r<5$, $\delta=1/100$, and
$t_s=1-p_s$. The ten poles are the ordered pairs
$(p_r,p_r+\delta)$; they are all less than $1/2$, and all queries
are greater than $1/2$. For a word $w\in\{-1,1\}^5$ and a shared
residue vector $c\in\mathbb R^{10}$ write
\[
 F_w(t)=\sum_{r=0}^4 w_r\left(
       \frac{c_r}{t-p_r}-\frac{c_{r+5}}{t-p_r-\delta}\right).
\]
\begin{proposition}
For
\[
 \sigma=(1,-1,-1,1,-1),\qquad
 \tau=(1,-1,1,-1,1),
\]
there are no nonnegative residues $c_r\geq0$ and separate real
biases $\beta_\sigma,\beta_\tau$ such that
\[
 w_s\bigl(F_w(t_s)+\beta_w\bigr)>0
 \quad\text{for all }s\text{ and both }w\in\{\sigma,\tau\}.
\]
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Eliminating each bias requires $F_w(t_a)>F_w(t_b)$ whenever
$w_a=1$ and $w_b=-1$. Since $t_s=1-p_s$, division by the
strictly positive factor $|p_a-p_b|$ rewrites this as $R_{w,a,b}c>0$,
where the first and second coordinates of each pole pair are
\[
 (R_{w,a,b})_r=
 \frac{\operatorname{sgn}(p_a-p_b)w_r}
 {(1-p_a-p_r)(1-p_b-p_r)},\qquad
 (R_{w,a,b})_{r+5}=
 \frac{-\operatorname{sgn}(p_a-p_b)w_r}
 {(1-p_a-p_r-\delta)(1-p_b-p_r-\delta)}.
\]
There are twelve comparison rows. Append the ten coordinate rows
$e_r^\top$, and call the resulting matrix $A$. The exact rational
vector in \texttt{positive-paired-root-q5-dual.json} has twenty-two
entries, eleven of them nonzero, and satisfies
\[
 \lambda\geq0,\qquad \sum_i\lambda_i=1,\qquad
 \lambda^\top A=0,\qquad \sum_{i<12}\lambda_i>0.
\]
All these identities are independently verified by
\texttt{positive-paired-root-q5-check.py}, which regenerates every
row from the displayed poles and words using exact rational arithmetic.
Under the proposed inequalities the first twelve entries of $Ac$
would be strictly positive and the remaining entries nonnegative.
Consequently $\lambda^\top Ac>0$, contradicting the exact zero
identity. This proves the proposition.
\end{proof}

The certificate rules out a geometry-independent theorem asserting
that every two root words admit a common nonnegative pole pool with
separate biases. It does not exclude signed residues, target-dependent
pole locations, a different ordered grid, or compatibility on the
specific hierarchical grids. Each individual root word is realizable
with strictly positive residues by the antisymmetric-numerator
construction in \texttt{butterfly-positive-root.tex}.
