\begin{theorem}[Exact repair with a constant factor]
Let $r,s\geq1$, and suppose
\[
 F(z,w)=c+\sum_{h=1}^H
 \frac{a_{h0}+\alpha_h\cdot z+\beta_h\cdot w}
 {c_h-p_h\cdot z-q_h\cdot w}
\]
has $p_{hi},q_{hj}>0$ and every denominator positive on the entire
Boolean $(r+s)$-cube. There exists a sum $G$ of $3H+3$ affine ratios,
all with strictly negative denominator slopes and positive denominators
on the entire cube, such that
\[
 G(0,w)=G(z,0)=0,\qquad G(e_i,e_j)=F(e_i,e_j).
\]
In particular, a universal strict reverse grid sign compiler of cost
$H(r,s)$ yields the axis-zero compiler required by the checked gluing
reduction at cost $3H(r,s)+3$. This preserves either requested logarithmic
or double-logarithmic saving. The theorem does not construct the remaining
unproved sublinear reverse grid compiler.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Whole-cube positivity gives $c_h>\sum_i p_{hi}+\sum_j q_{hj}$.
Put $k_h=a_{h0}/c_h$, and absorb the constant numerator by defining
\[
 T_h(z)=(\alpha_h+k_hp_h)\cdot z,\qquad
 W_h(w)=(\beta_h+k_hq_h)\cdot w,\qquad
 c'=c+\sum_h k_h.
\]
Then $F=c'+\sum_h(T_h+W_h)/B_h$, with the original denominators
$B_h=c_h-p_h\cdot z-q_h\cdot w$.
Choose $0<\eta_h<\min(\{p_{hi}\}_i\cup\{q_{hj}\}_j)$ and set
\[
 D_h=c_h-\eta_h(|z|+|w|),\quad
 U_h=(p_h-\eta_h\mathbf1)\cdot z,\quad
 V_h=(q_h-\eta_h\mathbf1)\cdot w.
\]
The three-ratio expression
\[
 Q_h=\frac{T_h+W_h}{D_h-U_h-V_h}
       -\frac{T_h}{D_h-U_h}
       -\frac{W_h}{D_h-V_h}
\]
vanishes on both Boolean axes. Every denominator has strictly negative
slopes. The two modified denominators are pointwise at least $B_h$ on
the cube, so positivity is retained. On the promised grid,
\[
 Q_h(e_i,e_j)=\frac{T_h(e_i)+W_h(e_j)}{B_h(e_i,e_j)}
 -\frac{T_h(e_i)}{c_h-p_{hi}-\eta_h}
 -\frac{W_h(e_j)}{c_h-\eta_h-q_{hj}}.
\]
Consequently $F(e_i,e_j)-\sum_hQ_h(e_i,e_j)=R_i+C_j$, where
\[
 R_i=c'+\sum_h\frac{T_h(e_i)}{c_h-p_{hi}-\eta_h},\qquad
 C_j=\sum_h\frac{W_h(e_j)}{c_h-\eta_h-q_{hj}}.
\]
These arbitrary row and column corrections themselves admit an exact
three-head, axis-zero realization. Put $L=2(r+s)+4$,
$E=L-|z|-|w|$, and
\[
 M_R(z)=(L-2)(L-3)\sum_iR_iz_i,\qquad
 M_C(w)=(L-2)(L-3)\sum_jC_jw_j.
\]
Define
\[
 P(z,w)=\frac{M_R(z)}{E-|w|}
       +\frac{M_C(w)}{E-|z|}
       -\frac{M_R(z)+M_C(w)}{E}.
\]
All slopes are $-1$ or $-2$. All denominators are at least
$L-2(r+s)=4>0$. The expression vanishes when either block is zero.
At $(e_i,e_j)$ its value is $R_i+C_j$, because
\[
 (L-2)(L-3)\left(\frac1{L-3}-\frac1{L-2}\right)=1.
\]
Thus $G=\sum_hQ_h+P$ has every asserted property. No limiting
perturbation or weakly admissible atom is used. The input dimension is
unchanged, and all identities hold exactly.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Weak reverse slopes cost no additional heads]
The theorem remains valid when input denominator slopes are merely
nonpositive. All output slopes are strictly negative, with the same
$3H+3$ bound and exact preservation of every grid value.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Write $n=r+s$ and let one input denominator be
$B(x)=b_0+\sum_kb_kx_k$ with $b_k\leq0$. Its full-cube minimum is
$\delta=B(\mathbf1)>0$. Set $\varepsilon=\delta/(n+1)$ and replace it by
\[
 B'(x)=B(x)+\varepsilon(2-|x|).
\]
Every slope is $b_k-\varepsilon<0$. Also
$B'(x)\geq\delta+\varepsilon(2-n)=3\varepsilon>0$ throughout the cube.
Every promised grid input has Hamming weight two, so $B'=B$ there.
Leave the numerators and scalar bias unchanged, then apply the strict
repair theorem. This is an exact finite gauge, not a limiting argument.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Verification status.}
The proof above is complete as a written algebraic construction.
The accompanying exact-rational check enumerates 3,600 cube evaluations
for $1\leq r,s\leq4$ and $0\leq H\leq3$, independently checking axes,
grid equality, and all denominators. This finite check is supplementary;
the universal proof is the displayed construction. The universal construction is checked in
\texttt{ReverseGridAxisRepair.lean}:
\texttt{strict\_reverse\_ratio\_axisize},
\texttt{strict\_axis\_row\_col\_repair}, and
\texttt{exists\_reverse\_axis\_repair}. The theorem
\texttt{hasReverseAxisCompiler\_of\_strictReverseGridCompiler}
exposes the exact cost transfer, and
\texttt{worstCaseHeadComplexity\_real\_le\_of\_strictReverseGridCompiler}
connects it to the unchanged global model. Independent axiom inspection
reports only standard Lean foundations: propositional extensionality,
classical choice, and quotient soundness. The weak-slope extension is
checked by \texttt{weak\_reverse\_ratio\_strictify},
\texttt{exists\_reverse\_axis\_repair\_of\_weak}, and
\texttt{hasReverseAxisCompiler\_of\_weakReverseGridCompiler}; an additional
900 exact rational cube evaluations check the finite gauge.
