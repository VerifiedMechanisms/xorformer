\paragraph{Status.}
The construction, exact grid identity, strict slopes, whole-cube positivity,
and both Boolean axis identities are Lean checked in
\texttt{Results/ReverseMaskedCauchy.lean}. This is an assembly theorem.
It does not claim that the resulting queries shatter a superlinear number
of columns, or establish either accepted asymptotic target.

\begin{theorem}
Let $D,\eta>0$, let $0<p_{hi}<t_j<1$, and choose arbitrary residues
$a_{hi}$ and fixed masks $M_{hj}\in\{0,1\}$. Suppose for each $h$ that
\[
 D\sum_i p_{hi}+s\eta+D\sum_jM_{hj}(1-t_j)<D+\eta.
 \tag{1}
\]
There is a sum of $2H$ affine ratios, with strictly negative denominator
slopes and positive denominators on the whole Boolean $(r+s)$-cube,
which vanishes on both Boolean axes and whose grid values are
\[
 (1-t_j)\sum_h\frac{M_{hj}a_{hi}}{t_j-p_{hi}}.
 \tag{2}
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For each pole use the two denominators
\[
 B_h^+(z,w)=D+\eta-D\sum_i p_{hi}z_i
       -\sum_j\bigl(\eta+DM_{hj}(1-t_j)\bigr)w_j,
 \qquad
 B_h^-(z,w)=D+\eta-D\sum_i p_{hi}z_i-\eta\sum_jw_j,
\]
and the opposite numerators $A_h$ and $-A_h$, where
\[
 A_h(z,w)=D\sum_i(1-p_{hi})a_{hi}z_i.
\]
All slopes are strictly negative. The largest sum of absolute slopes is
the left side of (1), so the denominators are positive on the full cube.
On $z=0$ each numerator is zero. On $w=0$ the two denominators are
equal, giving exact cancellation for every Boolean $z$.

At a grid point, a masked-out head cancels exactly. For a masked-in head
the contribution is
\[
 \frac{D(1-p)a}{D-Dp-D(1-t)}-
 \frac{D(1-p)a}{D-Dp}
 =\frac{(1-t)a}{t-p}.
\]
Summing proves (2). In particular this is an exact simple-pole Cauchy
kernel after residue rescaling, rather than an approximation by a
squared kernel. Since $t_j<1$, its column factor preserves all strict
signs. No separate axis repair or weak-slope perturbation is needed.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Lean declarations.}
\texttt{pairedRowsAxisData} constructs the more general pairwise
cancellation module with independently moving row poles.
\texttt{pairedRowsAxisData\_grid} proves its exact grid evaluation.
\texttt{paired\_cauchy\_identity} checks the rational identity, and
\texttt{exists\_maskedCauchy\_reverseAxisData} combines the identity
with the whole-cube budget. The output is the existing
\texttt{ReverseAxisData} used by the checked gluing reduction.
The original model and complexity definitions are unchanged.

\paragraph{The remaining requirement.}
To obtain a superconstant saving from this platform, one must still give
fixed masks and query times with a universal row decoder using
$o(s)$ moving poles, and satisfy (1) for all rows together.
Finite shattering certificates and pairwise cancellation alone do not
prove this assertion.
