\section{The repaired-face budget}
Write $C(a,b;M)$ for the existing Lean proposition
\texttt{HasReverseAxisCompiler a b M}, which requires a universal
strict reverse-orientation axis-zero compiler using at most $M$ heads.
The checked star-face transfer gives, for $a,b\geq1$,
\[
 C(a,b;M)\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 \W(a+b)\leq \frac{484\,2^{a+b}M}{ab}
       +\frac{11\,2^a}{a}+\frac{11\,2^b}{b}.
 \tag{1}
\]
The two last terms approximate arbitrary real residual values on the
exceptional faces using affine monomial-star covers. Strict margins on
the finite cube make approximation sufficient; the construction is
proved against the original admissible atoms. Its proof and exact
declaration are in \texttt{AxisZeroStarFaces.lean} and
\texttt{AxisZeroUnbalancedTransfer.lean}.

For $n\geq32$, choose $b=\lfloor\log_2 n\rfloor$ and $a=n-b$.
Then
\[
 1\leq b,\qquad a\geq n/2,\qquad n/2<2^b\leq n,
 \qquad n^3\leq2^n.
\]
Consequently
\[
 \frac{11\,2^a}{a}\leq\frac{44\,2^n}{n^2},
 \qquad
 \frac{11\,2^b}{b}\leq11n\leq\frac{11\,2^n}{n^2}.
 \tag{2}
\]
Thus the entire exceptional-face cost is at most $55\,2^n/n^2$.
In particular the earlier $2^a$ face cost is not an obstruction to a
logarithmic smaller block.

\begin{theorem}[Finite budget]
Let $n\geq32$, $K\geq0$, and $0<d\leq n$. If
$C(n-b,b;M)$ and $M\leq Kb/d$, then
\[
 \W(n)\leq (968K+55)\frac{2^n}{nd}.
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The main term in (1) is at most
$484K2^n/(ad)\leq968K2^n/(nd)$.
By (2) and $d\leq n$, the remaining terms are at most
$55\,2^n/(nd)$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Checked asymptotic implication]
Suppose that a constant $K\geq0$ satisfies, for all sufficiently large
$n$, the existence of an $M$ with
\[
 C(n-b,b;M),\qquad
 M\leq K\left(1+\frac{b}{\log b}\right),
 \qquad b=\lfloor\log_2 n\rfloor.
\]
Then $\W(n)=O(2^n/(n\log\log n))$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For $n\geq32$, $b\geq3$ and
$n<2^{b+1}$. Since $\log2<3/4$,
$\log n<(b+1)\log2\leq b$. Hence
$0<d:=\log\log n\leq\log b\leq b\leq n$ and
\[
 M\leq K(1+b/\log b)\leq 2Kb/d.
\]
The finite theorem gives the explicit eventual constant $1936K+55$.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{What is still missing.}
The checked linear compiler costs $b+1$ after transposing the grid.
To obtain the new implication's hypothesis, it must be improved to
$O(b/\log b)$ for all $(n-b)\times b$ sign tables with the required
full-cube positivity. No such construction has been proved. The smaller
block changes the quantitative compiler regime; it does not supply
the missing saving.
