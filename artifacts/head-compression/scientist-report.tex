\paragraph{Target status.}
This run has not proved that an absolute constant $C>0$ satisfies
$W(n)\le C2^n/n^2$ for every positive integer $n$. No Lean declaration
of that statement was added. No construction in the record should be
read as establishing it.

\paragraph{Formal results.}
The inventory lists $66$ principal and companion declarations across
$27$ new Lean modules. The full project build passed with $9211$ jobs.
The inspected declarations use only \texttt{propext},
\texttt{Classical.choice}, and \texttt{Quot.sound}.
Among the construction results are
\texttt{HeadComplexity.computableWithSharedHeadsN\_of\_sharedAffineScore}
in \texttt{Results/SharedAffineScoreLift.lean}, which transfers an
already strictly separating shared-affine score to $M+1$ actual heads;

\texttt{HeadComplexity.affineFraction\_pair\_merge\_error\_le}

error bound. The declarations in \texttt{Results/ReciprocalExchange.lean}
also verify an exact three-head cycle producing a checkerboard correction
on one face and zero correction on the opposite face. None asserts
universal existence of a small score.

\paragraph{Finite certificate.}
The file \texttt{certificates/random-n14-anchored-h292.json} gives
$292$ heads for one fixed fourteen-bit Boolean function. All $16384$
labels agree with the original trial target. An independent integer
audit verifies every strict sign and every denominator's whole-cube
admissibility. Its minimum margin exceeds $0.999999$.
Separate certificates for its original lower and upper faces use
$146$ and $147$ heads; their audited gluing uses $293$ heads.
These examples do not bound the maximum over fourteen-bit functions.

\paragraph{Unsuccessful routes.}
Fixed-denominator spanning, bounded-cancellation constructions,
local-cell packing, arithmetic memory decoding, dominant-term
constructions, and restricted confluent-jet constructions did not
produce the target bound. The writeup distinguishes proved obstructions
to specified subclasses from theorem-strength gaps in proposed general
constructions. Numerical failures and solver timeouts are never used
as lower bounds. The later pruning and denominator-coalescence outcomes
are recorded separately in the final continuation diagnostics.
