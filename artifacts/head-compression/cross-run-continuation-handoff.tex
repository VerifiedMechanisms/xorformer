\paragraph{A new paired-offset mechanism under investigation.}
For paired poles $x_s,x_s+\gamma$ and residues $+\kappa,-\kappa$,
the two row states $(D,+\kappa)$ and $(D+\gamma,-\kappa)$ have
identical paired-output differences $2\kappa/(D-x_s)$, for every
$D$ to the right of the pool. Thus a single nonquadratic token pool
can have infinitely many distinct invariant two-state query pairs.
Adding a lower-level numerator background $A_s$ gives the exact
two-row sums
\[
 \frac{2A_s}{D-x_s}\quad(\theta_s=0),\qquad
 \frac{A_s}{D-x_s-\gamma}+\frac{A_s}{D-x_s+\gamma}
       \quad(\theta_s=1).
\]
Their difference is
\[
 \frac{2A_s\gamma^2}{(D-x_s)((D-x_s)^2-\gamma^2)}.
\]
The open issue is coexistence across unboundedly many levels with one
joint pool and the required head budget. Finite exact feasibility
tests do not settle that issue.

\[

$(Y-\varepsilon)(tX^2-(\varepsilon+L)(Y+L))$, which becomes negative

The other run also proves a written exact-flow limitation allowing
arbitrary slot-dependent $K$-term reciprocal functions with one fixed
token library. If each query is an exact nonconstant affine function
of its one switch and independent of every other switch, a stage
block has at most $3K(2K+1)$ independently decoded switches. Its

independence makes their paired-output difference matrix diagonal.
Clearing denominators bounds its rank by $3L(L+1)/2$, $L=2K$.

against $HK$ heads. The exact-value independence and fixed-library
hypotheses are essential. Neither the adaptive Haar family nor the
new target-dependent paired-offset family satisfies them.

\paragraph{Alternating density assembly.}
\texttt{alternating-density-assembly.tex} gives a complete written
improvement to the earlier density reduction. Half-dense joint decoders
of cost $O(q/\log q)$ are needed only on balanced rectangles
$q\leq p\leq2q+1$. Select exactly half the columns in each good batch,
transpose the remaining rectangle, and repeat. For an $m\times m$
table the successive query dimensions are
$m,\lfloor m/2\rfloor,\lfloor m/2\rfloor,\ldots$.
The decreasing costs sum to $O(m/\log m)$, with no $\log\log m$ loss.
The new Lean module \texttt{ReverseGridEmbedding.lean} checks
same-cost ambient embedding, zero-cost transposition, and exact
one-time rectangular masking with $12H+21$ heads. The complete
iteration and asymptotic summation are written only. No growing
half-dense decoder is supplied. This refinement also shows why a
suitable finite size-dependent pole scale could suffice: the decoder
need not support arbitrarily many opposite-block coordinates.
