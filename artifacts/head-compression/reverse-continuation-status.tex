\paragraph{Research status.}
Neither accepted asymptotic target has been proved. In particular, no
unconditional $O(2^n/(n\log\log n))$ upper bound or
$\Omega(2^n/n)$ lower bound is claimed. The original model, input
dimension, atom admissibility, \texttt{HStar}, and
\texttt{worstCaseHeadComplexity} are unchanged. All logarithms in the
asymptotic statements are natural. This is a checkpoint during continuing
research, not a completion certificate.

\paragraph{Unconditional checked bounds.}
The imported and checked Warren proof, followed by
\texttt{Results/WarrenAsymptotics.lean}, gives for $n\geq16$
\[
 \frac{2^n}{70n^2}\leq W(n)\leq\frac{11\cdot2^n}{n}.
\]
The new lower-bound declarations are
\texttt{worstCaseHeadComplexity\_real\_ge\_two\_pow\_div\_seventy\_square}
and \texttt{two\_pow\_div\_square\_isBigO\_worstCaseHeadComplexity}.
The existing upper-bound declaration is
\texttt{worstCaseHeadComplexity\_real\_le\_eleven\_mul\_two\_pow\_div}
in \texttt{Results/UniversalLinearSaving.lean}. The factor of $n$
between these estimates remains open in this run.

\paragraph{A written improvement of the upper constant.}
\texttt{reciprocal-segre-degeneration.tex} proves the exact-value bound
\[
 W(n)\leq2\left\lceil\frac{2^n}{n+1}\right\rceil\qquad(n\geq1).
\]
This is a complete written deduction, not a Lean theorem. It uses the
established generic secant-dimension theorem for products of projective
lines by Catalisano, Geramita, and Gimigliano
(\texttt{arXiv:0809.1701}, Theorem 4.1), together with the inverse function
theorem. The exceptional third secant of the four-factor Segre variety
does not occur at the rank used here. A rescaled Boolean finite-difference
transform makes positive reciprocal features converge in first derivative
to Segre features. Full Jacobian rank then gives an open set of exact
tables; subtracting two tables in that set realizes every real table.
The primary theorem and this transfer were independently audited.
Exact modular Jacobian checks for dimensions one through six are only
finite checks of the formulas. This constant-factor improvement is not
either accepted asymptotic resolution.

\paragraph{The concrete gluing gap has been closed.}
\texttt{Results/AxisZeroTransfer.lean} proves the descending order of
cells, exact affine center substitutions, preservation of earlier strict
signs, and bias absorption. Its declaration
\texttt{worstCaseHeadComplexity\_real\_le\_of\_reverse\_axis\_compiler}
gives, for a local budget $M$ in dimensions $a,b\geq1$,
\[
 W(a+b)\leq\frac{484\cdot2^{a+b}}{ab}M+2^a+2^b.
\]
The cover transfer is in \texttt{AxisZeroCover.lean}; the exceptional-face
repairs are in \texttt{AxisZeroFaces.lean}. Thus there are
$O(2^n/n^2)$ local modules and an $O(2^{\lceil n/2\rceil})$
correction cost. The proof is instantiated with the checked linear
compiler in \texttt{AxisZeroBaselineTransfer.lean}.
\texttt{AxisZeroAsymptotics.lean} proves both desired asymptotic
\emph{implications}, under the corresponding compiler hypotheses.
Those hypotheses have not been discharged.

\paragraph{A logarithmic smaller block now has a checked complete budget.}
\texttt{AxisZeroStarFaces.lean} replaces each exponential exceptional-face
repair by the corresponding affine monomial-star cover. The resulting
bound in \texttt{AxisZeroUnbalancedTransfer.lean} is
\[
 W(a+b)\leq484\,2^{a+b}M/(ab)+11\,2^a/a+11\,2^b/b.
\]
For $b=\lfloor\log_2 n\rfloor$ and $a=n-b$,
\texttt{AxisZeroUnbalancedAsymptotics.lean} checks that the face terms
are at most $55\,2^n/n^2$. Its final declaration
\texttt{worstCaseHeadComplexity\_isBigO\_loglog\_of\_logarithmic\_block\_compiler}
therefore proves the desired upper asymptotic conditional on an
$O(b/\log b)$ universal compiler in these unbalanced dimensions.
This compiler is still unproved. The complete calculation is recorded
in \texttt{logarithmic-block-budget.tex}; its constants include every
module and correction head. The aggregate build and axiom audit pass.

\paragraph{Axis zeros and strict slopes are no longer separate obstacles.}
\texttt{Results/ReverseGridAxisRepair.lean} turns any $H$-head reverse
grid representation with positive full-cube denominators and weakly
nonpositive slopes into a strictly admissible axis-zero representation
with at most $3H+3$ heads, preserving every grid value. In particular,
\texttt{exists\_strict\_reverse\_grid\_of\_weak} restores strict slopes
without increasing the grid head count, and
\texttt{exists\_reverse\_axis\_repair\_of\_weak} supplies the full repair.
The still-missing ingredient is a universal reverse grid representation
with a superconstant saving.

\paragraph{Additional checked assembly tools.}
\texttt{Results/ReverseRankCorrection.lean} proves exact rank-$L$
grid correction with $L+1$ strictly admissible heads, including arbitrary
row, column, and constant terms. The principal declaration is
\texttt{exists\_strict\_reverse\_rank\_grid}.
\texttt{Results/ReverseRowFlip.lean} proves exact row multipliers from
$\{-1,0,1\}$ with $2H+2$ heads, the transposed column version,
and a row-flip plus column-mask construction with $4H+6$ heads.
Its density-to-compiler implication records a precise sufficient
condition; an efficient dense family is not proved to exist.

\paragraph{A checked exact Cauchy platform.}
\texttt{Results/ReverseBalancedRows.lean} constructs strictly admissible
axis-zero modules with balanced row numerators. Its declarations
\texttt{exists\_balancedRows\_atoms},
\texttt{exists\_pairedCauchy\_reverseAxisData}, and
\texttt{exists\_permutationCauchy\_reverseAxisData} establish exact paired
Cauchy readouts, with arbitrary row biases and an explicit full-cube
exterior margin. This verifies the analytic platform, not a growing
ratio of programmable rows to heads.

\paragraph{New full-cube identities and tangent spaces.}
\texttt{reciprocal-histogram-fibers.tex} gives complete elementary
written proofs of positive reciprocal representations with common
full-cube values. In a growing dimension, one fiber contains
$\exp(\Omega(nH\log n))$ distinct unordered representations and equally
many distinct tangent spaces in a specified parameter slice. The slice
has an invertible selected-output Jacobian at each permutation point.
This does not assert isolation when all atom coefficients vary, and
neither representation counts nor distinct tangent spaces imply
independent sign labels.

The basic one-triple invariance and its exact original-atom realization
are now Lean checked in \texttt{Results/ReciprocalHistogram.lean}, by
\texttt{reciprocal\_sum\_eq}, \texttt{atoms\_sum\_eq}, and the unconditional
concrete example \texttt{adjacent\_swaps\_atoms\_sum\_eq}.
The large fiber count is not Lean formalized.
\texttt{reciprocal-histogram-tangents.tex} supplies a complete finite
seven-point strict interpolation proof from two branches, with exact
rational certificates. Its enlarged first-order family on a fixed
$d$-dimensional filler face has the necessary condition
$2Hd+1\geq2^d$. Both statements have precisely restricted scope and
settle neither global target.

\paragraph{Written constructions with no superconstant saving.}
\texttt{reverse-joint-axis-sheets.tex} gives a complete written
constant-factor compiler using at most
$2\lceil(\min(r,s)+1)/3\rceil+3$ heads. It is not Lean formalized.
\texttt{reverse-joint-deformed-sheets.tex} and
\texttt{reverse-alternative-permutation-fibers.tex} construct many
regular permutation sheets. The latter uses actual Boolean cube
coordinates and strictly admissible atoms. Their factorially many
interpolation roots do not imply independently readable signs.
\texttt{reverse-alternative-moment-fibers.tex} proves exact
moment-preserving Stieltjes readouts, but at linear cost in the number of
independent readouts. These are complete written arguments, not either
accepted global result.

\paragraph{Audited limitations of proposed decoders.}
\texttt{reverse-common-pole-count.tex} proves a linear VC bound for a
family with one common moving pole and independent residues. It does
not cover independently moving poles.
\texttt{reverse-alternative-coalescing-flats.tex} proves a
backward-shift invariant for reciprocal-column limits and a linear
sign-capacity bound for graded limits with $O(\log H)$ profile
dimensions. It also exhibits a positive-pole nongraded limit, so
gradedness cannot be assumed. Exact small arrangement enumerations
in \texttt{reverse-joint-affine-planes.tex} show extra signs from
deformed sheets but do not establish a growing row/head ratio.

\texttt{reverse-joint-balanced-codebook.tex} proves that a common
relabeling and zero-residue padding resolve color multiplicities at
linear cost. Cross-block interference remains unproved.
\texttt{reverse-joint-exact-support-rank.tex} proves that clearing
$H$ affine grid denominators gives separation rank at most
$2^{H+1}-2$. Consequently exact support on $k$ disjoint bipartite
components needs $H\geq\log_2(k+2)-1$, even with arbitrary nonzero
amplitudes. This rules out a constant-cost exact-support replacement
for each Erd\H{o}s--Moser order pair. It does not rule out a joint
construction with interacting nonzero tails.

\texttt{paired-support-budget-audit.tex} strengthens that restricted
conclusion to nonuniform component counts and total separately compiled
head cost. Every universal exact-support assembly of those modules
requires $\Omega(m)$ total heads. Its approximate version states an
explicit uniform-error condition. Neither assertion covers joint
representations with unrestricted interacting tails.

\texttt{butterfly-rectangle-decoder.tex} gives independent switch bits
readable by assignment rectangles. The span theorem in
\texttt{butterfly-wavelet-span.tex} identifies exactly which Haar tensor
components can vary. The analytic one-kernel decoder is obstructed
when its residues are fixed across settings. The separate
\texttt{butterfly-tree-cauchy-audit.tex} treats adaptive residues in a
specified four-pole tree limit: the required partial-sum zero lies
outside the admissible query interval. Exact certificates cover the
finite test, but these are restricted decoder obstructions, not a
global lower bound.

\paragraph{Lower-bound mechanisms that did not close the gap.}
The checked polynomial sign count yields the inverse-square estimate
above. Counting normalized interpolation roots more sharply cannot be
justified from positivity alone: the independent permutation-fiber
construction supplies too many regular positive-chamber roots for the
proposed shortcut. Denominator order data do not determine all oriented
circuits, and normalized fixed-scale metric entropy does not by itself
control all strict orthants without a margin hypothesis. These are
scoped failures of arguments, not a proof that the matching lower bound
is false. No restriction to a subclass of atoms is used to claim a
lower bound for the unrestricted model.

\paragraph{Verification and external hypotheses.}
The aggregate \texttt{lake build HeadComplexity.Results.All} completed
successfully after importing the new assembly and Warren modules.
Audits of the central Lean declarations report only
\texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice}, and \texttt{Quot.sound}.
There are no new axioms or \texttt{sorry} in these proofs.
The written analytic constructions use standard finite-dimensional
calculus, linear algebra, and stated sign-count results; they are
explicitly distinguished from Lean proofs. The only path in this
checkpoint to the requested upper asymptotic still assumes the
unproved efficient local compiler (or an equivalent efficient dense
family). No complete written proof of either accepted target is
available.
