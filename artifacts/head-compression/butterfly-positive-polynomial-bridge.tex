\paragraph{Status and scope.}
This is a finite, exact rational certificate for one sixteen-pole configuration.
It is not a Lean proof and makes no claim about all sufficiently small pole
scales, adaptive pole choices, larger profile spaces, or either asymptotic
HeadComplexity target. No model definition is changed.

Use the four target words and the pole grid
$p_y=\sum_{j=0}^3y_j\epsilon^{2^j}$, $\epsilon=1/100$, saved in
\texttt{butterfly-positive-polynomial-bridge.json}. Let $K_i$ be the
strict residue cone of target $i$ after eliminating the separate Haar-block
biases, as in \texttt{butterfly-adaptive-haar-biased.tex}. Define
\[
 K_i^+=K_i\cap(0,\infty)^{16},\qquad
 C_{ij}=\frac1{1-p_i-p_j},\qquad
 V_2=C^{-1}\operatorname{span}\{\mathbf1,(p_i)_i,(p_i^2)_i\}.
\]
All six intersections $K_i^+\cap K_j^+$, $i<j$, are nonempty.
Nevertheless,
\[
 (K_0^+\cap K_1^+)\cap
 \bigl((K_2^+\cap K_3^+)-V_2\bigr)=\varnothing.       \tag{1}
\]
The analogous signed intersection is nonempty by
\texttt{butterfly-polynomial-profile-bridge.tex}. Thus positivity cannot
be added to that finite signed repair without further argument.

\paragraph{Exact verification.}
For every pair, the JSON file saves a rational vector with sixteen strictly
positive entries. The checker reconstructs every bias-eliminated comparison
and verifies that it is strictly positive on the corresponding pair's vector.
This proves all six pair intersections directly.

For (1), write $V=C^{-1}[\mathbf1,p,p^2]$ and use variables
$(x,\lambda)\in\mathbb R^{16}\times\mathbb R^3$. A comparison row $r$
from cones $0,1$ becomes $(r,0)$; a row from cones $2,3$ becomes $(r,rV)$.
Append the sixteen coordinate rows asserting $x>0$ and the sixteen rows
asserting $x+V\lambda>0$. Let $A$ be the resulting rational matrix.
The saved certificate has twenty positive weights, extended by zero to a
vector $\eta$ on all rows, satisfying exactly
\[
 \eta\geq0,\qquad \sum_i\eta_i=1,\qquad \eta^\top A=0.
\]
If every required inequality held strictly, then
$\eta^\top A(x,\lambda)>0$, a contradiction. The checker uses exact
rational matrix inversion and fraction arithmetic, and no optimization solver:
\begin{verbatim}
python3 artifacts/head-compression/butterfly-positive-polynomial-bridge-check.py
\end{verbatim}

\paragraph{What remains open.}
This leaves pairwise positive compatibility, larger affordable query-profile
spaces, and target-adaptive pole perturbations as separate questions. The
certificate gives no lower bound for unrestricted HeadComplexity.
