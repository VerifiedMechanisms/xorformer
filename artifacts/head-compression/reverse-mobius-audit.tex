This note audits a specific proposed transfer from positive-orientation
Cauchy grids. It does not obstruct a joint reverse compiler or imply an
asymptotic head bound. The identities and inequalities below are complete
written proofs; they have not been formalized in Lean.

\begin{proposition}[Additive numerator covariance]
Let $T(t)=(\alpha t+\beta)/(\gamma t+\delta)$,
$\Delta=\alpha\delta-\beta\gamma\ne0$, and
$f(t)=\alpha-\gamma t$. Put $x=T(d)$ and $y=T(u)$.
Whenever all displayed denominators are nonzero,
\[
 \frac{A+a}{d-u}
 =\frac{A f(x)^2+a f(y)^2}{\Delta(x-y)}
  +\frac{\gamma}{\Delta}\bigl(Af(x)-af(y)\bigr).
\]
Thus applying the same projective change to every row pole and column
token preserves the additive numerator class modulo a row-only and a
column-only correction.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The inverse transformation gives
$d-u=\Delta(x-y)/(f(x)f(y))$.
Also $f(y)-f(x)=\gamma(x-y)$, whence
\[
 (A+a)f(x)f(y)-Af(x)^2-af(y)^2
 =\gamma(x-y)\bigl(Af(x)-af(y)\bigr).
\]
Division proves the identity. On a one-hot grid the new row and column
numerators, as well as the two corrections, are tabulated affine data.
Corrections from multiple heads add into a single row function and a
single column function. The three-head row--column repair in
\texttt{ReverseGridAxisRepair.lean} can handle their sum once the new
Cauchy denominators themselves have reverse cube extensions.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Three-by-three extension criterion]
Let $R_1,R_2,R_3$ and $C_1,C_2,C_3$ satisfy $R_i-C_j>0$.
A positive affine denominator with nonpositive slopes on the six-bit
cube and grid values $R_i-C_j$ exists if and only if
\[
 G=\sum_i R_i-\sum_j C_j-2\max_iR_i+2\min_jC_j>0.
\]
The same criterion permits strictly negative slopes.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Every additive representation has the form
$B(z,w)=K-L-\sum_i(K-R_i)z_i-\sum_j(C_j-L)w_j$.
Nonpositive slopes require $K\ge\max_iR_i$ and
$L\le\min_jC_j$. Its cube minimum is
$\sum_iR_i-\sum_jC_j-2K+2L\le G$.
Conversely, $K=\max R$ and $L=\min C$ attain $G$ with weak slopes.
If $G>0$, increasing $K$ and decreasing $L$ by sufficiently small
positive amounts makes all slopes strictly negative while retaining
positivity.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[A precise failure of projective compactification]
Suppose $0<a<b<c$, $c\ge a+b$, and $1/a\ge1/b+1/c$.
Take row poles $d_i\in\{a,b,c\}$ and column tokens
$u_j\in\{-a,-b,-c\}$. No real projective map $T$, finite on these
six points, can simultaneously have
\begin{enumerate}
\item $T(d_i)-T(u_j)>0$ for all nine pairs; and
\item an affine reverse denominator positive on the entire six-bit cube
with these exact grid values.
\end{enumerate}
In particular, the concrete six-point configuration $a=1,b=2,c=4$
already fails.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Adding a constant to all transformed coordinates and multiplying them by
a positive scalar preserve both conditions. An affine increasing $T$
therefore has extension criterion proportional to
$2(a+b-c)\le0$.

For a nonaffine projective map, write its pole as $p$. Positivity of every
row--column difference forces either $p\notin[-c,c]$ and, after the
invariances just noted, $T(t)=1/(p-t)$; or $p\in(-a,a)$ and
$T(t)=1/(t-p)$. A pole within either of the two point ranges gives both
signs among the row--column differences, so cannot satisfy the first
condition.

In the outside case, $T$ is increasing on the six points. The criterion
from the lemma is
\[
 G=\frac{2a}{p^2-a^2}+\frac{2b}{p^2-b^2}
       -\frac{2c}{p^2-c^2}.
\]
For $0<x\le c$ and $|p|>c$,
\[
 \frac{x}{p^2-x^2}\le\frac{x}{c}\frac{c}{p^2-c^2}.
\]
Thus $G\le2((a+b)/c-1)c/(p^2-c^2)\le0$.

In the separating-gap case, $T$ is decreasing within each cluster.
The maximum row value is $T(a)$ and the minimum column value is $T(-a)$.
Consequently
\[
 G=-\frac{2a}{a^2-p^2}+\frac{2b}{b^2-p^2}
       +\frac{2c}{c^2-p^2}.
\]
For $x\ge a>|p|$, cross multiplication gives
\[
 \frac{x}{x^2-p^2}\le\frac{a}{x}\frac{a}{a^2-p^2}.
\]
Hence $G\le2(-1+a/b+a/c)a/(a^2-p^2)\le0$.
Both cases violate the necessary strict positivity of the cube minimum.
\end{proof}

The conclusion is limited to one common projective transformation of a
single Cauchy denominator grid. It allows arbitrary additive numerators
and the covariance correction, but does not cover changing the target
grid values by a joint sum of heads, introducing additional heads,
partitioning the grid, or using a nonprojective construction. Therefore
it rules out an automatic projective transfer of exponential ranking
ranges, without ruling out the requested local compiler.
