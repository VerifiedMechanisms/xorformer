\paragraph{Scope.}
These are written algebra and one exact rational finite certificate.
They do not give an asymptotic lower bound. The common-row Cauchy
two-token obstruction does not apply to the head-dependent anchor
arguments considered here. A bounded exploratory search was stopped
after generic compact probes, a perturbative Cauchy family, and a
broader positive-denominator family produced no parity reader. The
certificate below is independent of floating-point infeasibility tests.

Let $D_{ih}$ be fixed anchor arguments, let $Q_h$ be fixed query
arguments, and let $q_h(s)$ be token parameters indexed by a finite
state set. Assume every displayed denominator is positive and each
anchor matrix $M(s)_{ih}=1/(D_{ih}-q_h(s))$ is invertible. Given
arbitrary fixed anchor numerators $A_{ih}$, solve
\[
 \sum_h\frac{A_{ih}+c_h(s)}{D_{ih}-q_h(s)}=0
 \qquad(1\leq i\leq H).
\]
The query has arbitrary fixed numerators $a_h$ and a bias:
\[
 F(s)=b+\sum_h\frac{a_h+c_h(s)}{Q_h-q_h(s)}.
 \tag{1}
\]

\begin{proposition}[Numerator-space dimension]
For fixed arguments and token states, the vectors $(F(s))_s$ form
a linear space of dimension at most $H^2-H+2$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
There are $H^2+H+1$ numerator and bias parameters. For each head,
adding a constant $k_h$ to all its anchor and query numerators and
subtracting $k_h$ from its solved residue leaves all ratios unchanged.
These give $H$ independent kernel directions.

There are a further $H-1$ kernel directions: choose numbers
$\alpha_h$ with $\sum_h\alpha_h=0$, set
$A_{ih}=\alpha_hD_{ih}$ and $a_h=\alpha_hQ_h$, and choose
$c_h(s)=-\alpha_hq_h(s)$. Every summand is then $\alpha_h$, so
all anchors and the query are zero. These directions are independent
of the first $H$. Indeed, if their sum agrees with a constant
headwise numerator direction, then
$\alpha_hD_{ih}=k_h=\alpha_hQ_h$ at all rows. For any nonzero
$\alpha_h$, that column of the anchor matrix is constant across
rows, and its query value equals that same constant. If several
such columns occur, the anchor matrix is singular. Thus at most one
$\alpha_h$ is nonzero; its sum-zero condition makes it zero as well.
The kernel therefore has dimension at least $2H-1$, giving the bound.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[A concrete four-head failure]
Put $e=1/1000$, and let $p$ range over all 24 permutations of
$(1,2,3,4)$. Set $q_h(p)=ep(h)$,
$D_{ih}=1-eR_{ih}$, and $Q_h=1-eT_h$, where
\[
 R=\begin{pmatrix}8&1&9&4\\8&1&1&1\\2&5&9&5\\7&3&7&3\end{pmatrix},
 \qquad T=(6,1,8,5).
 \tag{2}
\]
No choices of the fixed numerators and bias in (1) give the strict
full-permutation parity signs on all 24 states.
The five row profiles and all 24 memory states have a simultaneous
original reverse-grid realization whose denominators exceed $9/10$
on the whole Boolean cube.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The exact script \texttt{audit\_noncauchy\_anchor\_parity.py} constructs
the $24\times21$ rational feature matrix $E$ of (1). If
$v_h(p)=1/(Q_h-q_h(p))$ and $w(p)=v(p)^{\mathsf T}M(p)^{-1}$,
its anchor-numerator entry is $-w_i(p)M(p)_{ih}$, its query-numerator
entry is $v_h(p)$, and its bias entry is one.

Rational elimination verifies that $E$ has rank 14 and constructs
a vector $z$ satisfying
\[
 99/100<z_p<101/100,\qquad
 E^{\mathsf T}\operatorname{diag}(\operatorname{sgn}p)z=0.
 \tag{3}
\]
The construction is reproducible without a linear-programming
solver: row-reduce the transpose of any exact column basis of $E$
after multiplying its columns by parity, set all free unknowns to
one, and solve for the pivot unknowns. The script verifies (3)
against all 21 original feature columns with exact rational arithmetic.
If every parity-weighted score were positive, its inner product with
the positive vector $z$ would be positive, contradicting (3).

On five row bits $x_i$ and 24 memory bits $y_p$, the denominator
of head $h$ is
\[
 1-e\sum_{i=1}^4R_{ih}x_i-eT_hx_5-e\sum_p p(h)y_p.
\]
All slopes are strictly negative. Its minimum is at the all-one
vertex; the least of the four minima is $453/500>9/10$. Hence the
usual exact affine-ratio parameter conversion applies in the unchanged
model. The certificate is about this one fixed reader, not every
four-head reader or every non-Cauchy anchor choice.
\end{proof}
