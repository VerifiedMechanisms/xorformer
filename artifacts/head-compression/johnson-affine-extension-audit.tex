\paragraph{Status.}
These are complete elementary written proofs of extension and localization
facts. They do not give a compiler, a covering theorem, or an improved
worst-case head bound. They are not Lean formalizations. In particular,
the obstruction below concerns preservation of a specified affine
denominator, not every alternative ratio representation of its score.

Let $S$ and $T$ be disjoint coordinate sets, of sizes $k,r\geq1$.
The swap grid consists of the points $S\setminus\{i\}\cup\{j\}$,
where $i\in S$ and $j\in T$. Suppose prescribed denominator values are
$D_{ij}=b+u_i+v_j$.

\begin{proposition}[Exact one-orientation extension criterion]
An affine denominator with strictly positive slopes and positive constant
coefficient extends $D$ to the whole Boolean $(k+r)$-cube if and only if
\[
 b+\sum_{i\in S}u_i-(k-1)\max_i u_i+\min_jv_j>0.
\]
An everywhere-positive affine denominator with strictly negative slopes
extends $D$ if and only if
\[
 b+\min_i u_i+\sum_{j\in T}v_j-(r-1)\max_jv_j>0.
\]
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Write the extension as $\alpha+\sum_i A_ix_i+\sum_jB_jx_j$.
The grid identities imply, and are implied by, the existence of two
scalars $\lambda,\mu$ such that
\[
 A_i=\lambda-u_i,\qquad B_j=\mu+v_j,\qquad
 \alpha=b+\sum_i u_i-(k-1)\lambda-\mu.
\]
Indeed differences between two row indices determine all $A_i$ up to
one additive constant, and column differences do the same for $B_j$.
For positive slopes one needs $\lambda>\max u$ and
$\mu>-\min v$. The greatest possible limiting constant coefficient is
exactly the first displayed expression. Strict positivity of that
expression is necessary and sufficient, choosing both parameters close
to their strict bounds. This remains valid when $k=1$.

For negative slopes one needs $\lambda<\min u$ and
$\mu<-\max v$. The minimum cube value occurs at the full set and equals
\[
 \alpha+\sum_iA_i+\sum_jB_j
 =b+\lambda+(r-1)\mu+\sum_jv_j.
\]
Its greatest possible limiting value is the second expression. The same
strict approximation argument proves necessity and sufficiency,
including $r=1$.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Mixed local slopes on a paired diagonal]
Suppose $c+\sum_{i=1}^m d_i z_i$ is positive on the Boolean $m$-cube.
On the paired diagonal $x_{a_i}=1-z_i$, $x_{b_i}=z_i$ it is the
restriction of an affine denominator with positive constant coefficient
and strictly positive slopes on the Boolean $2m$-cube.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Positivity is equivalent to
$c>\sum_i\max(0,-d_i)$. Choose $A_i>\max(0,-d_i)$ with
$\sum_iA_i<c$, and set $B_i=A_i+d_i>0$ and
$\alpha=c-\sum_iA_i>0$. Then
\[
 \alpha+\sum_i A_ix_{a_i}+\sum_i B_ix_{b_i}
 =c+\sum_i d_iz_i
\]
on the paired diagonal. Every numerator affine in $z$ also extends,
by replacing $z_i$ by $x_{b_i}$. This uses the existing $2m$ coordinates;
it makes no statement allowing new coordinates in the original model.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Affine localization cannot isolate a swap grid]
Let $q$ be affine and nonnegative on a Boolean cube. If it vanishes on a
set $E$, then it vanishes on the smallest coordinate face containing $E$.
In particular, a paired diagonal cannot be isolated by such a penalty.
For $k,r\geq2$, a swap grid on $S\cup T$ cannot be isolated either:
its smallest coordinate face is the whole cube on these coordinates.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Write $q(x)=a+\sum_i c_ix_i$. Its cube minimum is
$a+\sum_i\min(0,c_i)\geq0$. Any zero is a minimizer, so at a coordinate
with $c_i>0$ every zero has $x_i=0$, and at a coordinate with $c_i<0$
every zero has $x_i=1$. Every coordinate taking both values in $E$
therefore has $c_i=0$. On the minimal coordinate face the remaining
coordinates are fixed, so $q$ is constant there and that constant is zero.
Every paired coordinate takes both values. On a swap grid with
$k,r\geq2$, every coordinate does too.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{The exact gluing gap.}
On the Hamming layer of size $k$, the affine expression
$(k-1)-\sum_{i\in S}x_i$ is zero on the swap grid and positive at points
at swap distance at least two from $S$. It is negative at $S$ itself.
Adding an arbitrarily large positive multiple to a denominator therefore
violates positivity at $S$. The paired-diagonal extension lemma does not
supply the nonnegative localization penalty needed by the already checked
reverse-axis gluing theorem. No new cell ordering, repair budget, or
superconstant saving is asserted here.
