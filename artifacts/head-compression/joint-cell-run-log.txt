Early decisive checks
The finite-plane line sums depend on all memory tokens on each line.
Their common incidence geometry does not share arbitrary independent table
values. Rank-head denominators may be fixed while their column numerators
vary. Cauchy point-correction denominators vary with the table's tokens.
Uniform axis choices make those point corrections' row repairs fixed;
the rank repairs retain a label-dependent aggregate column vector.

For k blocks of m one-hot coordinates, M = m^k and n = km. Slicing into
two-dimensional tables has local cost of order m^(k-1)/log m, so its
efficiency is only m log m, below n log n. Moreover, taking products of
the inherited star-cover bound introduces a 22^k cover constant. Passing
the cell-size entropy test alone therefore does not establish a saving.

The direct finite-plane affine-selector extension has an additional rank
obstruction: its nonvertical line-incidence matrix has real rank q^2-q+1.
Binary addresses do not preserve arbitrary affine line selectors in a
smaller ambient dimension. A formal proof is being developed separately.
