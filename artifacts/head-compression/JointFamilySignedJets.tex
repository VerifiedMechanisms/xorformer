\title{Shared polynomial jets in arbitrary signed row statistics}
\maketitle

\paragraph{Checked positive primitive.}
A polynomial of degree at most $R$ in an arbitrary signed linear row
statistic is a pointwise limit of a bank of $R+1$ admissible reverse
cube--star heads. Each column may choose its polynomial independently.
Every approximating denominator is positive throughout the full Boolean
cube, every local slope is strictly negative, and the column axis is
exactly zero. In particular, adding this polynomial correction to any
prior numerical score preserves every specified strict sign for some
finite admissible bank. This is kernel checked in
\texttt{JointFamilySignedJets.lean}, principally
\texttt{jointFamily\_signed\_polynomial\_limit} and
\texttt{jointFamily\_signed\_polynomial\_correction\_preserves\_signs}.

\paragraph{Exact formula and limit.}
Let $V(x)=\sum_i v_i x_i$, with $v_i$ unrestricted. For $h=0,\ldots,R$, set
\[
 u_i=R|v_i|+1,\qquad w_{hi}=u_i+h v_i>0,
 \qquad p_h(x)=U(x)+hV(x),\qquad U(x)=\sum_i u_i x_i.
\]
The weights $\lambda_{rh}$ are the coefficient of $X^r$ in the Lagrange
basis polynomial of node $h$ among the nodes $0,\ldots,R$. Hence
\[
 \sum_h\lambda_{rh}p_h(x)^\ell=0\quad(\ell<r),
 \qquad \sum_h\lambda_{rh}p_h(x)^r=V(x)^r.
\]
The exact Cauchy moment remainder gives
\[
 T^{r+1}\sum_h\frac{\lambda_{rh}}{T-p_h(x)}
  =\sum_h\lambda_{rh}p_h(x)^r\frac{T}{T-p_h(x)}
  \longrightarrow V(x)^r.
\]
For independent column polynomials $P_j$, all powers reuse the same bank:
\[
 a_{hj}(T)=\sum_{r=0}^R [X^r]P_j\,T^{r+1}\lambda_{rh}.
\]
On the whole row--column cube the actual numerator and denominator are
\[
 N_h(x,z)=\sum_j a_{hj}(T)z_j,\qquad
 D_h(x,z)=T+1-\sum_iw_{hi}x_i-\sum_jz_j.
\]
Taking $T>\sum_i(2R|v_i|+1)+s$ proves positivity everywhere. The limit
argument permits divergent residues; no uniform boundedness assumption
is made or needed. It preserves finitely many strict signs by pointwise
convergence. It does not assert exact equality to the polynomial at a
finite value of $T$.

\paragraph{Linear cost for exceptional rows.}
For any row $x_0$, Hamming distance $d(x,x_0)$ is an affine signed
statistic with values $0,\ldots,k$, zero only at $x_0$. The degree-$k$
Lagrange basis of zero therefore represents its point indicator.
Consequently an arbitrary correction at one row, with independent
amplitudes in all columns, costs $k+1$ heads while preserving all required
prior signs. A family of $m$ row corrections costs $m(k+1)$.
These statements are checked in \texttt{JointFamilyLinearRepair.lean} as
\texttt{jointFamily\_point\_correction\_preserves\_signs} and
\texttt{jointFamily\_point\_family\_correction\_preserves\_signs}.
The earlier quadratic bank has exact zero values away from the point;
this linear bank instead uses a limit and preserves all specified strict
signs. Both have an exact zero column axis.

\paragraph{Weak-learning charge.}
For $M=2^k$ rows and $s$ independent columns, squared error $E$ leaves at
most $E$ rows with an incorrect or zero sign. Their repair now costs
$O(Ek)$ heads. A block weak learner of cost $L$ and squared normalized
correlation $\gamma^2$ would give the total bound
\[
 O\left(\frac{L}{\gamma^2}\log\frac{Ms}{E}+Ek\right).
\]
Taking $s\asymp k$ and $E=M/k^2$ reduces the logarithm to $O(\log k)$
and the repair term to $O(M/k)$. The missing uniform gain remains
$\gamma^2/L\gg\sqrt{k}\log k/M$ for the square-root benchmark.
No such gain is established by the polynomial-jet primitive alone.

\paragraph{A failed radial localization estimate.}
A different proposed block was $d/(\varepsilon+d)^p$, concentrated near
the first Hamming layer about one corner. In the limit
$\varepsilon\to0$, that layer has squared energy $k$, whereas the central
layer alone contributes
\[
 \binom{k}{\lfloor k/2\rfloor}\lfloor k/2\rfloor^{2-2p}.
\]
For even $k$, the elementary bound
$\binom{k}{k/2}\geq2^k/(k+1)$ shows that central-layer energy at most $k$
requires
\[
 p\geq1+\frac{k-\log_2(k(k+1))}{2\log_2(k/2)}.
\]
Thus $p=O(\log k)$ does not localize this block in Frobenius norm on the
full cube. This rejects that particular concentration estimate, not a
weighted-domain construction or arbitrary signed multihead cancellation.
