\paragraph{Status and scope.}
Exact rational certificates and guarded rational-function continuation,
not Lean proofs. The fixed unbiased routing fails already at sixteen
wires, even with unrestricted signed residues. The analogous thirty-two
wire word succeeds with signed residues and legal independent block
biases for every sufficiently small pole scale. Neither result is a
universality theorem or an obstruction in the original HeadComplexity
model.

For $H=2^d$ use
\[
 p_y(\epsilon)=\sum_{j=0}^{d-1}y_j\epsilon^{2^j},\qquad
 t_{k,s}=1-p_s(\epsilon).
\]
At every switch $e=(k,s,g)$ prescribe and route the bit
\[
 \theta_e=\left\lfloor s/2^{\max(0,k-1)}\right\rfloor\bmod2,
 \qquad \sigma_e=1-2\theta_e.
\]
Every switch is retained. Let $\pi_\theta$ be the resulting butterfly
permutation and let $u_{k,g}$ be the standard signed input-block Haar
function. Define
\[
 A_e(c,\beta)=\sigma_e\left[
 \sum_h\frac{u_{k,g}(h)c_{\pi_\theta(h)}}
 {1-p_s-p_{\pi_\theta(h)}}+\beta_{k,g}\right].
\]
All denominators are positive for sufficiently small positive $\epsilon$.

\begin{theorem}[Failure without biases]
For $d=4$, no real residue vector $c$ makes every $A_e(c,0)$ positive,
when $\epsilon=1/100$, or for any sufficiently small positive $\epsilon$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The file \texttt{butterfly-signed-unbiased-h16-dual.json} gives a
normalized strictly positive dependence on the seventeen query rows
\[
 5,6,11,12,16,19,20,21,23,24,25,26,27,28,29,30,31.
\]
There are no residue nonnegativity rows. The checker verifies the
positive weights, their unit sum, and their exactly zero weighted row
sum at $\epsilon=1/100$. Thus it excludes arbitrary real residues.

Keeping this support, solve the sixteen column equations and unit-sum
equation over $\mathbb Q(\epsilon)$. The guarded series calculation
certifies a nonsingular square system and positive leading coefficients
for all seventeen solution entries. Their vanishing orders lie between
zero and twenty-nine. Therefore the normalized dependence remains
strictly positive throughout some punctured interval
$0<\epsilon<\epsilon_0$. Its positive weighted sum excludes the strict
query system throughout that interval.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Success with block biases]
For $d=5$, there are real residues and independent block biases making
all eighty $A_e(c,\beta)$ positive, when $\epsilon=1/100$, and for every
sufficiently small positive $\epsilon$. Both residues and biases may
depend on $\epsilon$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
There are $32+31=63$ variables. The file
\texttt{butterfly-signed-biased-h32-germ.json} specifies a square basis
of forty-nine signed query equations with right side one and fourteen
coordinate-zero equations. At $\epsilon=1/100$, exact rational solution
and substitution give all eighty signed scores at least one. Eighteen
of the thirty-two residues are negative.

The same basis defines a rational-function solution. Its nonsingularity
and all eighty positive score leading coefficients are certified by
exact power series arithmetic with explicit precision guards. The
largest pivot order is seventy-four. To use only power series, the
checker first multiplies the right side by $\epsilon^{384}$ and later
subtracts that exponent when reporting the score orders. Every division
uses a certified nonzero leading coefficient, and every final basis
residual is certified zero beyond order $384$. The calculation thus
computes genuine leading coefficients of the unique rational-function
solution, rather than evaluating a small floating-point parameter.

Each of the finitely many nonzero score functions has the sign of its
positive leading coefficient on a sufficiently small positive interval.
Take the minimum of these finitely many thresholds and the denominator
positivity threshold. Dividing the common scaled solution by the
positive $\epsilon^{384}$ gives the asserted strict solution.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Reproducible verification.}
Run \texttt{heavy python3 artifacts/head-compression/}
\texttt{butterfly-signed-bias-stress-check.py}. The checker uses no
feasibility solver. It reconstructs the finite basis solutions and all
matrices independently from the saved routing, then verifies the guarded
series certificates. These are computational proofs of the two stated
finite-dimensional rational-function claims. They do not establish a
uniform construction as $d$ grows. In particular, the unbiased failure
must not be presented as a failure of the biased or auxiliary-routing
families.
