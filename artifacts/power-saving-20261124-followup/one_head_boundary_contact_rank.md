# Boundary contacts constrain new relevant coordinates

## Status and scope

The edge and affine-maximizer lemmas are kernel checked in `HeadComplexity/Research/OneHeadBoundaryContacts.lean`. The rank bound and the first-order boundary application below are written proofs. This result concerns one head and a common fixed unit-contact simplex. It does not bound all branches of that simplex and does not improve unrestricted head complexity.

## Edge lemma

Let $A=a_0+\sum_i a_i x_i$ and $B=b_0+\sum_i b_i x_i$, where every coefficient of $B$ is strictly positive. Suppose $f(x)A(x)\geq B(x)$ throughout the cube. If the label flips along coordinate $i$, at an edge with zero endpoint $x$, then

$$ |a_i|\geq B(x)+B(x+e_i)=2B(x)+b_i\gt b_i. $$

Consequently $|a_i|\leq b_i$ makes coordinate $i$ irrelevant to the truth table. This statement includes equality, which is useful when a contact equation forces $a_i=\pm b_i$.

Lean checks `abs_slope_gt_of_opposite_gap` and `coordinate_irrelevant_of_abs_slope_le`. The ordinary fact that a maximizing Boolean vertex has bit one at every strictly positive affine slope is `maximizing_coordinate_of_positive_slope`.

## A conditional rank bound

Partition the coordinates into $k$ fixed coordinates $z$ and $m$ free coordinates $x$, with $n=k+m$. Let $F$ be the face $z=0$. Suppose a positive coherent affine ratio has a full-cube unit gap and an affinely independent family of $n+1$ unit contacts. Assume that every fiber with $z\neq0$ has a constant truth-table label, possibly depending on $z$. Let $\ell$ of the contact points belong to $F$.

Let $R$ be the free coordinates that are relevant to the truth table restricted to $F$, and write $r=|R|$. Then

$$ r\leq\ell+1. $$

**Proof.** Put $U=(A+B)/2$ and $V=(B-A)/2$. Relevance and the edge lemma give $|a_i|\gt b_i$ for every $i\in R$, so the slopes $U_i$ and $V_i$ are both nonzero and have opposite signs.

At an outside negative contact, the entire fiber has negative labels, hence $U\leq0$ on that fiber and $U=0$ at the contact. Its projection onto $R$ must therefore be the unique maximizing pattern $p$ of the slopes $U_i$. At an outside positive contact, the same argument with $V$ forces the complementary pattern $1-p$.

Project the augmented contact rows to the coordinates $(1,z,x_R)$. All outside rows lie in the linear space generated by a constant row, the $k$ fixed-coordinate rows, and the single indicator distinguishing the two patterns $p$ and $1-p$. Their rank is at most $k+2$. Adding the $\ell$ inside rows raises rank by at most $\ell$. Restoring the omitted $m-r$ free-coordinate columns raises rank by at most $m-r$. Affine independence of all contacts therefore gives

$$ n+1\leq k+2+\ell+m-r=n+2+\ell-r, $$

which proves the assertion. No claim is made that the rank bound is sharp. $\blacksquare$

## Application to a straight common-zero boundary path

Consider affine coefficient paths $A_t=A_0+tA_1$ and $B_t=B_0+tB_1$ for all sufficiently small $t\gt0$. Suppose all paths have positive coherent denominators, full-cube unit gap, and the same $n+1$ independent unit contacts and their labels. Suppose $B_0$ vanishes exactly on $F$, and $A_0$ also vanishes on $F$. Finally assume $B_1|F$ has a strictly positive constant and strictly positive free-coordinate slopes.

Since $A_0$ vanishes on $F$, it depends only on $z$, as does $B_0$. The limiting unit gap gives $|A_0(z)|\geq B_0(z)\gt0$ at every outside fiber. For all sufficiently small $t$, each such fiber has the same label at every $x$. On $F$, the ratio is exactly $A_1/B_1$. The preceding rank argument applies, giving at most $\ell+1$ relevant coordinates in that restricted truth table.

This rules out a proposed amplification that places only one or a few unit contacts on $F$ and tries to obtain arbitrary high-dimensional threshold behavior there. It does not apply if $A_0$ fails to vanish on $F$, if the outside fibers already have varying labels, or if the fixed contact simplex is changed. Higher-order and multiple-scale paths require a separate analysis.
