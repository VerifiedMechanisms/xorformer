# Research Audit and Unresolved Target

## Target status

The current eventual fixed-exponent target is unproved. It asks for fixed $\varepsilon,c\gt0$ and $n_0$ with

$$ W(n)\geq c\frac{2^n}{n^{2-\varepsilon}}\qquad(n\geq n_0). $$

The original unboundedness target remains unproved as well. The manuscript's architecture, unrestricted real parameters and embedding dimensions, target-dependent denominators, and definitions of head complexity have not been changed. None of the auxiliary statements below is presented as completion of the target.

The unconditional Warren proof imported verbatim from branch `ar/1c1556983291` now builds in this worktree, including `HeadComplexity.worstCaseHeadComplexity_real_ge_two_pow_div_seventy_square`. It proves $W(n)\geq2^n/(70n^2)$ for $n\geq16$. All thirteen declarations in the combined baseline and latest Boolean-difference axiom audit use only the standard foundational axioms. This establishes the verification status of the baseline, not an improvement of its exponent.

The [address-budget audit](critical-scale-address-audit.md) checks both factorial constructions at the critical head scale, with exact integer ranges. Their component multiplicity does not refute the proposed critical-scale sign count. That full-class count remains a theorem-strength gap.

The asker's [critical-width capacity lower bound](critical-width-capacity-lower.md) is now audited as a complete written actual-model construction. It excludes a full-table upper count of order $nH$ in that range, and is compatible with the candidate order $nH\log n$. It is neither a worst-case head lower bound nor a VC lower bound of the same order.

The latest permutation-family audit separates [generic fixed-level endpoint nonvanishing](permutation-moment-divisibility.md) from [distinct exceptional levels with linear Taylor depth](distinct-level-deep-cancellation.md). The latter construction disproves every uniform sublinear truncation in head count, even on two input bits. Both proofs are written and supplemented by exact finite arithmetic. They do not prove the unrestricted target and have not been formalized in Lean.

## Approaches that did not establish the target

The [research ledger](research.md) records the manuscript audit, primary literature checks, explicit counterexamples, and blocked steps. The unresolved steps include a uniform sign count with adaptive denominators, a valid bound on canonical minimum branches, and a realization theorem converting free reciprocal queries or independent quadratic jets into the actual architecture at the required head cost.

Generic coordinate uniqueness, uniformly small critical-point counts, a head-independent polynomial degree, and several monotonicity or hyperbolicity relaxations fail for the explicit reasons in the linked audits. Fixed-denominator counts, bounded-weight neural-network theorems, finite-field phases, and exact tensor-value ranks do not cover the requested unrestricted sign representations.

## Numerical work is not a proof

[Continuation diagnostics](continuation-diagnostics.md) distinguishes the certified small-margin construction from its uncertified continuation. The continued branch lost a necessary multiplier sign far below unit margin. Independent stationarity replays rejected solver-reported successes. Corrected searches use rescaled objectives, stable coherent-denominator formulas, all cube constraints, and a separate head-table energy objective.

`experiments/late-search-diagnostics.json` retains search outcomes and hashes. Finite failures and missing numerical candidates imply neither infeasibility nor the desired asymptotic lower bound. `run.json` records the target status, checked declarations, and actual timing.

- [General row-scaling geometry](row-scaling-geometry-audit.json): twenty-four Lean-checked finite-dimensional matrix declarations, including singular-Laplacian solvability and uniqueness of the least-norm tangent. No head-count lower bound.

- [Fixed-direction flattening obstruction](homothetic-flattening-audit.json): eleven checked declarations and an exact rational replay. Two admissible heads realize the original labels, but the specified flattened denominator pair cannot. Adaptive direction changes and both asymptotic targets remain open.

- [Sparse-sign compiler obstruction](sparse-sign-compiler-audit.json): eleven Lean declarations, including `HeadComplexity.HStar_affineTriple_le`; complete written general construction and spectral proof. Both standard polynomial dictionaries fail to provide a polynomial-size universal conversion. This is not a worst-case head lower bound.

- [Tensor sign conversion audit](tensor-sign-conversion-audit.md): a complete written obstruction to a constant-cost conversion, using the earlier checked bipartite construction and Warren's theorem. The weaker sufficient conversion remains unproved; neither asymptotic head target is established.

- [Boolean difference kernel](boolean-difference-kernel.md): five Lean-checked identities, signs, exclusion, and actual-atom realization; general positive-weight and order-two determinant formulas proved in writing.
- [Failure of higher-order total positivity](difference-kernel-total-positivity.md): complete written number-theoretic obstruction and concentration transfer, with independent exact arithmetic certificates for concrete minors. No asymptotic head lower bound.
